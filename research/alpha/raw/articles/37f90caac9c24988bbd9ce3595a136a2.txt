To celebrate the growing Alpha token ecosystem and reward our most active traders, we’re launching a limited-time Binance Alpha Points Promotion starting 2025-05-01 00:00 (UTC).
Promotion Period: From 2025-05-01 00:00 (UTC) - 2025-09-04 00:00 (UTC)
During the Promotion Period, users can earn extra Alpha Points through eligible trades. Specifically:
BSC Alpha token purchases or

Limit order purchases of Alpha tokens (on any network) on Alpha 2.0

will be double-counted toward users’ Alpha trading volume.
If purchased with:&nbsp;
both BSC Alpha token and limit buy orders on Alpha 2.0

will be counted four times toward users’ Alpha trading volume.
Alpha Volume Formula:
Alpha Volume is calculated based on the type of purchase, with different weights applied:
* 4x purchases that use BOTH the BSC Alpha token and the Alpha 2.0 limit order feature
* 2x purchases that use EITHER the BSC Alpha token or the Alpha 2.0 limit order feature
* 1x all other eligible purchases
Example:
Instant buy $100 of non-BSC token ABC

Instant buy $110 of BSC token XYZ

Limit buy $120 of non-BSC token ABC

Limit buy $130 of BSC token XYZ

Alpha Volume = 100 + 110*2 + 120*2 + 130*4 = 1080
User can earn 10 points.
